## GNU Unifont Loading-Screen Subset

The five Chinese glyphs in `assets/farmers_future_delight/textures/gui/loading/chinese_1_20_6.png` are unaltered bitmap glyphs from GNU Unifont 15.1.04, as distributed with Minecraft 1.20.6. This subset is used only by the loading screen. The compiled font is available under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, or the GNU GPL with the font embedding exception. The upstream license and copyright notices are included verbatim in `assets/farmers_future_delight/textures/gui/loading/UNIFONT-LICENSE.txt`. The ellipsis in that texture comes separately from Minecraft's bitmap font.
